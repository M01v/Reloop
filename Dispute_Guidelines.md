# ReLoop — Dispute Resolution Guidelines (v3.2)

Internal reference for anyone reviewing a buyer/seller dispute. Apply in order; if more than one
applies, the earliest one that clearly fits wins.

1. **If tracking shows the item was never actually delivered** — lost in transit, never picked up
   by the carrier, or delivered to a different address than the one on file at ship time —
   refund the buyer.

2. **If the seller acknowledges a listing or fulfillment error** — wrong item, wrong size,
   mislabeled condition — refund the buyer.

3. **If delivery is confirmed** — a valid signature, or credible evidence the buyer received the
   item (including the buyer's own messages) — and the dispute claims non-receipt, side with the
   seller.

4. **If the evidence genuinely conflicts or isn't enough to decide confidently, escalate for
   manual review.** Guessing is worse than saying so.

5. **Minor issues the two parties have already resolved between themselves in chat** don't need
   to be reopened or re-litigated.

6. **A pattern in someone's history is a reason to look more carefully at a case — not a reason
   to skip the evidence actually in front of you.**
